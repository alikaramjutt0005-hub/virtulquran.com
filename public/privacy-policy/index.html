Privacy Policy

Please read carefully before using our services.

Virtual Quran respects your privacy. When you contact us, book a free trial, or submit a form on our website, we may collect information such as your name, student name, country, phone number, email address, selected course, student age, time zone, message, IP address, and submission date.

When you use our trial form, your country is detected automatically from your connection using ipapi.co, so the phone field shows the correct dialling code. This shares your IP address with ipapi.co for the lookup only.

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange trial classes, manage student communication, provide Quran learning services, and improve our support process.

We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted service providers such as website hosting, email delivery, and communication tools only where needed to operate our services.

Your information is kept only for as long as needed for communication, enrolment, support, record keeping, or legal/business requirements.

You can contact us at learnquranonlinetutors@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information.
