Quran Games for Kids

19 short games to practise the Arabic letters, joined letters, the Qaida marks, duas, salah, the order of the short surahs and Islamic basics. Each game takes two or three minutes. Play together with your child and collect stars.

How these games help your child

Children remember what they enjoy. These games turn the first lessons of the Quran into short, happy practice: recognising the Arabic letters and their dots, knowing a letter when it is joined in a word, saying the letter names in Qaida order, naming the marks such as zabar, zer and pesh and reading the sound they make, knowing which letters are heavy, learning which dua to read at each moment of the day, and remembering the order of the short surahs and the Islamic months.

Use them as a reward after a lesson, or for five minutes before bedtime. They work best alongside our free online Noorani Qaida, Daily Duas and Tajweed Rules.

Tips for parents

  • Sit with younger children and ask them to say each letter name out loud as they tap.
  • Keep it short. Two games a day is plenty.
  • Praise effort, not only stars.
  • The games check recognition. Correct pronunciation still needs a teacher who listens.

The games are free, with no ads and no sign-up. Stars are saved only on your own device.
