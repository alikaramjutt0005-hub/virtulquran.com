    Tips for teachers

    Small habits that keep nazra students moving forward.

    • Save right after classIt takes ten seconds while the lesson is fresh, and the record stays exact.
    • Repeat before moving onMark a lesson Needs work and read it again next class. A strong Qaida makes the Quran easy.
    • Watch the attendanceTwo absences in a week is the moment to call the parents, before the habit breaks.
    • Send the weekly reportParents who see progress every week support the class at home.
    • Add a dua each weekOne short dua or kalima a week adds up to a full set by the end of the year.
    • Back up every weekDownload a backup, so a new phone or a cleared browser never loses your students' records.

    Questions

    Nazra Tracker FAQ

    Is the Nazra Tracker free?

    Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up, no ads and no limits. Teachers, parents and madrasahs can all use it.

    Where is the record saved?

    Only in the browser on the phone or computer you use. Nothing is sent to our website or to anyone else. Use Download backup now and then, so you can move the records to a new phone or restore them if the browser is cleared.

    Can I track students in the Qaida and in the Quran?

    Yes. Each student is either in the Qaida or reading the Quran. For the Qaida you save the takhti, the page and the lines read. For the Quran you save the surah and the ayahs. When a student finishes the Qaida, move them to the Quran with one tap.

    How does the attendance work?

    Each day you mark a student Present, Late, Absent or On leave, and the minutes of the class. The Attendance page shows the whole month with the days present, absent and on leave, and the total minutes.

    Can I add duas, kalimas and Islamic studies?

    Yes, if you wish. In each class you can add any of the 46 daily duas, the six kalimas, short surahs learned by heart, salah and wudu, and Islamic studies topics. They appear in the reports too.

    How do I send a report to parents?

    Open the student and tap Weekly report or Monthly report, then save it as a PDF or print it. The report carries only the student's record and your note, with no website name, so you can send it as your own. You can also send today's class on WhatsApp.

    Can a teacher use it for a whole class?

    It works well for a family or a small group, up to about ten students, on one device. Download a backup every week. Records live in this browser only, so a teacher with many students, or several teachers sharing a class, will need a school system with logins, which this free tool is not.

    Does it work offline?

    Once the page has opened, the tracker works without the internet, and everything stays on the device.
