Ramadan Guide

1234567
1

Step 1 of 7Sunnah

Wake up for suhur

Eat the pre-dawn meal, even if it is only a date and some water. It is a sunnah and full of blessing. Stop eating before true dawn.

2

Step 2 of 7Fard

Make the intention

Intend in your heart that you are fasting today for Allah. Getting up for suhur with the fast in mind is itself an intention.

3

Step 3 of 7Fard

Pray Fajr

Pray Fajr on time. The fast has now begun and lasts until sunset.

4

Step 4 of 7Fard

Guard the fast

Do not eat or drink. Also keep the tongue from lying, backbiting and arguing, and the eyes and ears from what is wrong. If someone quarrels with you, say: "I am fasting."

5

Step 5 of 7Sunnah

Fill the day with good

Pray all five prayers on time, read the Quran every day, give charity and help at home. Good deeds are rewarded many times over in Ramadan.

6

Step 6 of 7Sunnah

Break the fast at sunset

As soon as the sun sets, break the fast with dates or water and say: "Dhahabaz-zama'u wabtallatil-uruqu wa thabatal-ajru in sha Allah." Make dua, because the dua of a fasting person at iftar is accepted.

7

Step 7 of 7Sunnah

Pray Isha and Tarawih

After Isha, pray twenty rak'at of Tarawih, in sets of two. Then pray Witr. Try to hear the whole Quran in Tarawih during the month.

Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic year, the month in which the Quran was revealed. This is a day of fasting, step by step. The rulings follow the Hanafi school.

Who Must Fast

Fasting in Ramadan is fard.

  1. Every adult, sane Muslim must fast.
  2. Children are encouraged to practise so that they are ready when they grow up.
  3. A sick person or a traveller may delay the fast and make it up later.
  4. A very old or permanently ill person who cannot fast gives fidyah: food for one poor person for each fast.

What Breaks the Fast

Each of these breaks the fast.

  1. Eating or drinking on purpose. In a Ramadan fast this needs both qada and kaffarah.
  2. Water going down the throat by mistake while rinsing the mouth. This needs qada only.
  3. Eating after dawn thinking it is still night, or before sunset thinking the sun has set. This needs qada only.
  4. Vomiting a mouthful on purpose.

What Does Not Break the Fast

The fast stays valid.

  1. Eating or drinking out of forgetfulness. Stop as soon as you remember.
  2. Using a miswak.
  3. Vomiting without meaning to.
  4. Swallowing your own saliva.
  5. Applying oil, kohl or perfume.
  6. Taking a bath to cool down.

Laylatul Qadr

The Night of Power is better than a thousand months.

  1. Look for it in the odd nights of the last ten: the 21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th and 29th.
  2. Spend the night in prayer, Quran, dhikr and dua.
  3. Say this dua often: "Allahumma innaka afuwwun tuhibbul-afwa fa'fu anni." O Allah, You are Forgiving and love to forgive, so forgive me. (Jami at-Tirmidhi 3513)

I'tikaf

Staying in the masjid for worship.

  1. It is a sunnah for the last ten days of Ramadan. If some people of an area do it, the duty is fulfilled for all.
  2. It begins before sunset on the 20th and ends when the moon of Eid is seen.
  3. The person stays in the masjid and leaves only for real needs, such as the toilet and wudu.
  4. A woman makes i'tikaf in the place of prayer in her home.

Sadaqatul Fitr and Eid

How the month ends.

  1. Sadaqatul Fitr is wajib on every Muslim who owns the nisab, for himself and his young children. It is given before the Eid prayer.
  2. On the morning of Eid: take a bath, use the miswak, wear your best clothes and use perfume.
  3. Eat something sweet, such as dates, before going to the Eid prayer.
  4. Go by one road and return by another, saying the takbir quietly on the way.
  5. The Eid prayer is two rak'at with six extra takbirs, followed by the khutbah.

These rulings follow the Hanafi school. For a personal question, please ask a qualified scholar.
