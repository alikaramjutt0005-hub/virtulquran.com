      How it works

      Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil

      The three parts of a hifz day, as taught in most madrasahs, and how the tracker keeps each one.

      Sabaq

      The new lesson

      A few new lines or ayahs, memorised and recited to the teacher without looking.

      In the trackerChoose Sabaq, pick the surah and the ayahs. It remembers where your child stopped and suggests the next ayah tomorrow.

      Sabqi

      Recent revision

      The lessons of the last few days, or the current para, read every day so they stay strong.

      In the trackerLog it as Sabqi. The last sabaq surah is picked for you.

      Manzil

      Older revision

      Paras memorised earlier, revised in turn so that nothing already learned is forgotten.

      In the trackerThe Revision page lists every surah not revised for a week, oldest first. You can add whole paras in one go.

      How did it go?

      Mark every lesson. Surahs marked Needs work stay on the Revision page until they are revised again.

      Tips for parents

      Hifz is built one small day at a time. These habits help most children keep going.

      • Same time, same placeShort and daily beats long and rare. Ten calm minutes after Fajr or before bed is plenty for a young child.
      • Never skip manzilMost forgetting happens in the paras that are not revised. Keep a little manzil every day, even on busy days.
      • Listen before classHear your child's sabaq before the teacher does, even if you cannot read well yourself. Just follow along.
      • Praise the habitPraise the effort and the days in a row, not only the number of ayahs memorised.
      • Keep a teacherThe tracker counts ayahs. Correct recitation still needs a teacher who listens to every word.
      • Back up the recordDownload a backup now and then, so a new phone or a cleared browser never loses your child's progress.

      Questions

      Hifz Tracker FAQ

      Is the Hifz Tracker free?

      Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up, no ads and no limits. Anyone can use it: parents, students, teachers and madrasahs.

      Where is my child's record saved?

      Only in the browser on the phone or computer you use. Nothing is sent to our website or to anyone else. Use Download backup now and then, so you can move the record to a new phone or restore it if the browser is cleared.

      What are sabaq, sabqi and manzil?

      Sabaq is the new lesson memorised today. Sabqi is the revision of the most recent lessons, usually the last few days or the current para. Manzil is the revision of older memorisation, so that nothing already learned is forgotten.

      My child has already memorised some surahs. How do I add them?

      Open your child's dashboard and go to Quran map. Choose a para and tap Mark memorised next to each surah, or Mark whole para memorised. For part of a surah, tap Some ayahs and type the ayah numbers.

      Can I track more than one child?

      Yes. Tap Add another child. Every child on the device appears on the first screen; tap a name to open that child's dashboard.

      Can a teacher use it for a whole class?

      It works well for a family or a small group, up to about ten students, on one device. Download a backup every week. Records live in this browser only, so a teacher with many students, or several teachers sharing one class, will need a school system with logins, which this free tool is not.

      Can a teacher see the progress?

      The tracker keeps the record on your device only. To share it, use Send today's report on WhatsApp on the dashboard, or Print report to make a PDF for the teacher.

      Why does my mushaf start a para at a slightly different ayah?

      The tracker uses the standard 30-para division. In some 13-line and 15-line Indo-Pak copies, a few paras (such as 4, 7, 11, 14, 20 and 21) begin one to four ayahs earlier or later. Your child's memorised ayahs are counted exactly; only the para line between them may differ slightly.

      Does it work offline?

      Once the page has opened, the tracker works without the internet, and everything stays on the device.
